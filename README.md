## Problem Title
SIH 26033: Multiple intermediaries reduce farmers earnings and increase consumer prices.

## Problem Creater's Organization
Ministry of Consumer Affairs, Food & Public Distribution

## Theme
Agriculture, FoodTech & Rural Development

## Problem Description
Expected Solution: Create a digital marketplace that:

• Connects farmers/FPOs directly with consumers and bulk buyers.
• Provides logistics support.
• Uses AI for demand forecasting and route optimization.

Benefits:

• Better prices for farmers.
• Lower prices for consumers.
• Reduced supply chain inefficiencies.

## Proposed Solution
We propose FARMCONNECT, an AI-powered digital marketplace that directly connects farmers, FPOs, retailers, and consumers.

Our platform includes:
- Direct Selling: Farmers can sell crops directly to buyers without unnecessary middlemen.
- AI Price Assistant: Suggests suitable selling prices based on market demand.
- Smart Harvest Match: Connects farmers with buyers before harvesting.
- Shared Transportation: Combines nearby orders to reduce delivery costs.
- Crop Tracking: Provides information about crop origin, quality, and delivery.
## Technical Approach
Technologies Used:
- Frontend: HTML, CSS, JavaScript, React.
- Backend: Python, FastAPI.
- Database: PostgreSQL.
- AI/ML: Python, Scikit-learn.
- Mapping: Google Maps API.
- Cloud: Firebase or AWS.

## Feasibility and Viability
The platform can initially be implemented in a small agricultural community and expanded gradually.

Challenges:
- Limited digital knowledge among farmers.
- Changing market prices.
- Transportation difficulties.
- Maintaining product quality.
Solutions:
- Regional language and voice support.
- Updated market information.
- Shared transportation.
- Delivery tracking and quality verification.
